Robotics & embodied intelligence机器人与具身智能

Gaojing Zhang张高境

I’m a PhD student at the University of Sussex and a visiting doctoral researcher at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I study how robots follow language instructions, learn new skills, and complete long-horizon tasks.

我在英国苏塞克斯大学攻读博士，同时在上海交通大学开展博士访问研究。我研究如何让机器人理解语言指令、学习新技能并完成长时程任务。

Research interests研究方向

Vision–language navigation视觉语言导航

Language-guided navigation in outdoor environments, with hierarchical planning, local control, and persistent memory.

面向室外环境的语言引导导航，结合分层规划、局部控制与长期记忆。

Robot skill learning机器人技能学习

Acquiring and reusing manipulation skills for long-horizon tasks, including laboratory automation.

面向长时程任务的操作技能习得与复用，包括实验室自动化。

Multimodal robot policies多模态机器人策略

Connecting language and visual reasoning with low-level robot control through multimodal representations and model adaptation.

利用多模态表征和模型适配，将语言与视觉推理连接到底层机器人控制。
